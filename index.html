Common AC Problems

Is Your AC Acting Up?

Here are the most common AC problems we see in Mumbai homes. Use this as a general guide — but remember, every AC is different. A proper inspection is the only way to know for sure.

AC Running but Not Cooling

Usually a gas (refrigerant) leak or severely clogged filters. Sometimes both. The AC runs but the room stays warm.

Needs gas check + filter inspection

Water Dripping Inside

Almost always a blocked drain pipe or dirty drain pan. Very common in Mumbai's humidity. Can damage walls if ignored.

Needs drain cleaning

Strange Noise from AC

Rattling, clicking, or loud humming usually means a loose fan blade, worn motor bearing, or debris stuck in the unit.

Needs mechanical inspection

AC Not Turning On

Could be a tripped breaker, failed capacitor, PCB fault, or wiring issue. Don't keep pressing the remote — call us.

Needs electrical diagnosis

Foul or Musty Smell

Mold and bacteria growth inside the unit — very common in Mumbai's wet climate. A deep clean with anti-bacterial spray usually fixes it.

Needs deep cleaning

High Electricity Bill

A dirty AC works harder and uses more power. Regular servicing (every 3–6 months) keeps it efficient and your bill in check.

Needs routine service

Not sure which issue you have? That's perfectly fine — just describe what you're seeing and we'll figure it out together. A proper in-person inspection is always more reliable than a guess.

We believe the best way to build a business is through honesty — not flashy claims. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Our reputation in this neighbourhood is everything. We're not here to do a quick job and disappear.

We inspect first, then explain what's wrong and what it will cost. You'll never get a surprise bill. If the repair isn't worth it, we'll tell you that too — honestly.

We don't use cheap duplicate parts to save a few rupees. If we're replacing something in your AC, it's because it needs to be replaced — and with the right part.
